01Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Outwall (the "game") on Android and iOS, published by the developer of Outwall ("we", "us"). It describes the information that is handled when you play, why, and how you can control it.

The game is played offline against the computer. We do not run our own servers for player accounts, we do not collect analytics of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

02Information we handle

Information you give us

None. The game has no registration, login, chat or profile. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Game data on your device

Your progress (journey levels, stars, coins, unlocked characters, daily challenge streak, statistics), settings (sound, vibration, language, reminders) and purchase entitlements such as "Remove Ads" are saved on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or using Settings › Reset progress deletes it.

Information collected automatically by Google AdMob

When the game shows ads, Google AdMob (see section 7) may collect:

03How it is used

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded videos, and to give you the reward you chose to watch for.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To show local reminders, only if you allow them.
  • To detect fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU or UK), we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the game for everything else.

04Advertising

Outwall is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded videos are always optional. You choose to watch one for an in-game reward such as coins or a second chance in a match.
  • Banner and interstitial ads may appear outside of matches and at natural breaks, never during your first games. Buying Remove Ads removes them.
In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Limit ad tracking on your device

  • Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads) › Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

05In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases such as coin packs, a Starter Pack, Remove Ads and a VIP Pack. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction ID) so the item can be delivered and restored. We never receive your name, card number or billing address.

Purchases can be disabled or protected with a password in your store settings, which we recommend for devices used by children.

06Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device, for example when a new daily challenge or free gift is ready. These reminders are created on your device; no data is sent to us or to a push service. You can turn them off in Settings › Reminders or in your device settings.

08Storage and retention

Game data on your device is kept until you reset your progress, clear the game's data or uninstall the game. Information held by Google or Apple is kept for the periods described in their privacy policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you and are then deleted.

09Children

Outwall is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child's information was collected, ads for that player will be limited to non-personalised ads and we will delete any information we hold. Parents can contact us at any time.

10Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for targeted ads.

  • Delete your game data: use Settings › Reset progress, clear the game's storage, or uninstall the game.
  • Stop personalised ads: change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy options, or reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Data held by Google: since we do not hold identifiers ourselves, use the links in section 7, or contact us and we will help.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. They rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

12Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum and work only with established partners. All ad traffic is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the game and its data.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its partners or the law change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. For important changes we will also let you know in the game.
