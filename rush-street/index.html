01Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Rush Street (the "game") on Android and iOS, published by the developer of Rush Street ("we", "us"). It describes the information that is handled when you play, why, and how you can control it.

We designed the game to need as little information as possible. We do not run our own servers for player accounts, and we do not sell personal information.

02Information we handle

Information you give us

None. The game has no registration, login, chat or profile. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Game data on your device

Your progress (shops, upgrades, currencies, missions), settings (sound, language) and purchase entitlements such as "No Ads" are saved in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information collected automatically by partners

03How it is used

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded videos, and to give you the reward you chose to watch for.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To understand how the game is played, balance the economy and fix crashes.
  • To detect fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU or UK), we rely on your consent for personalised advertising and on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the game for everything else.

04Advertising

Rush Street is free to play and is supported by ads:

  • Rewarded videos are always optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward such as cash, a booster or a free spin.
  • Interstitial ads may appear at natural breaks, never during the tutorial. Buying No Ads removes them.
In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings. Without consent you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Limit ad tracking on your device

  • Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads) › Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

05In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases such as gem and cash packs, a starter pack, No Ads and an Auto Collector. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction ID) so the item can be delivered and restored. We never receive your name, card number or billing address.

Purchases can be disabled or protected with a password in your store settings, which we recommend for devices used by children.

07Storage and retention

Game data on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information held by our partners is kept for the periods described in their privacy policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you and are then deleted.

08Children

Rush Street is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child's information was collected, ads for that player will be limited to non-personalised ads and we will delete any information we hold. Parents can contact us at any time.

09Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for targeted ads.

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Stop personalised ads: change your consent in the game, or reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Partner data: since we do not hold identifiers ourselves, use the partner links in section 6, or contact us and we will help.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. They rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

11Security

We keep the information we handle to a minimum and work only with established partners. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the game and its data.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its partners or the law change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. For important changes we will also let you know in the game.

13Contact

Questions, requests or concerns about your privacy in Rush Street? Write to us using the developer email shown on the game's Google Play or App Store page. We usually reply within a few days.
